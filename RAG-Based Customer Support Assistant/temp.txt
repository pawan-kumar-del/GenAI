Section 1: Company Overview & Products
1.1 About NovaTech Solutions
NovaTech Solutions is a B2B and B2C SaaS company providing cloud-based project management, team
collaboration, and AI-powered productivity tools. Founded in 2015 and headquartered in Austin, Texas,
NovaTech serves over 2 million users across 80 countries. Our mission is to make teams more productive
through intelligent automation and seamless collaboration.
1.2 Product Portfolio
NovaTech Core (Free / Starter)
Basic project management, up to 5 users, 5 GB storage, kanban boards, task tracking.
NovaTech Pro (Paid — Individual/Team)
Advanced project management, unlimited tasks, 50 GB storage, time tracking, integrations with Slack, Jira,
GitHub.
NovaTech Business (Paid — Business)
All Pro features plus advanced reporting, 200 GB storage, priority support, SSO, audit logs, custom workflows.
NovaTech Enterprise (Custom Pricing)
All Business features plus dedicated infrastructure, 99.99% SLA, on-premise deployment, custom contracts,
dedicated success manager.
NovaTech AI Assistant (Add-on)
AI-powered task suggestions, auto-scheduling, meeting summaries, and smart notifications. Available as
add-on for Pro and above.
NovaDocs (Bundled / Standalone)
Collaborative document editor with version history, templates, and export to PDF/Word. Bundled with Business
and Enterprise plans.
NovaAnalytics (Add-on / Enterprise)
Advanced BI dashboards, custom KPI tracking, data exports, team performance heatmaps.
1.3 Supported Platforms
 Web App: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (latest 2 versions supported)
 Desktop: Windows 10/11, macOS 12+, Ubuntu 20.04+
 Mobile: iOS 15+, Android 10+ (native apps available)
 Browser Extension: Chrome and Firefox
 API: RESTful API and Webhooks (Pro plan and above)

2.2 Billing Cycles
All paid plans are billed monthly or annually. Annual billing provides a 20% discount over monthly billing. Prices
listed above are per-user per-month for monthly billing.
2.3 Plan Upgrades and Downgrades
 Upgrades take effect immediately; billing is prorated for the remainder of the current cycle.
 Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.
 When downgrading, users must ensure data usage stays within new plan limits before cycle end.
 Exceeding storage limits triggers a 7-day grace period; after that, uploads are blocked until resolved.
 Enterprise pricing requires a custom quote from the sales team. Contact sales@novatech.io.
2.4 Free Trial Policy
NovaTech Pro and Business plans offer a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required for the trial. At the end of
the trial, accounts automatically revert to the Core (Free) plan unless payment information is added. Data is
retained for 30 days after trial expiry before deletion. Enterprise trials are available on request and last 30 days.
Section 3: Account Management
3.1 Account Creation
Users can sign up at app.novatech.io using an email address and password, or via Google / Microsoft OAuth.
Business and Enterprise accounts support domain-based SSO. After registration, users receive a verification
email which must be confirmed within 48 hours.
3.2 Password and Authentication
 Minimum password length: 8 characters; must include uppercase, lowercase, and a number.
 Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) is available via Authenticator App or SMS.
 2FA is mandatory for Business and Enterprise plan admins.
 Password reset via 'Forgot Password' link; reset link valid for 1 hour.
 After 5 failed login attempts, accounts are temporarily locked for 30 minutes.
 Session timeout: 24 hours of inactivity on web; 7 days on mobile.

3.4 Account Deletion
Account owners can delete their account from Settings > Account > Delete Account. Deletion is irreversible. All
data is permanently removed within 30 days per GDPR requirements. Business and Enterprise accounts
require email confirmation to support@novatech.io before deletion. If a billing cycle is active, any unused days
are refunded as per the refund policy.
Section 4: Billing & Payments
4.1 Payment Methods
 Credit and Debit Cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover.
 PayPal: Available for monthly plans only.
 ACH Bank Transfer: Available for annual Business and Enterprise plans.
 Wire Transfer: Enterprise only, minimum $5,000/year.
 Invoicing: Available for Business (annual) and Enterprise customers.
4.2 Invoice and Receipts
Invoices are automatically generated and emailed to the billing contact on each renewal date. Past invoices are
available in Settings > Billing > Invoice History. Invoices include itemized charges, tax information, and a unique
invoice number. Custom purchase order (PO) numbers can be added for Enterprise customers.
4.3 Failed Payments
If a payment fails, NovaTech will retry the charge on days 3, 7, and 14 after the initial failure. The account
owner receives email notifications at each retry. After 14 days without successful payment, the account is
downgraded to the Core (Free) plan. Data is preserved for 30 days. To reactivate, update payment information
in Settings > Billing.
4.4 Taxes
NovaTech charges applicable sales tax based on the billing address. US customers in applicable states are
charged state sales tax. EU customers are charged VAT. Tax-exempt organizations must submit a valid
exemption certificate to billing@novatech.io.
4.5 Proration Rules
 Mid-cycle upgrades: Charged prorated amount for upgrade immediately.
 Mid-cycle user additions (Business/Enterprise): Charged per-user prorated daily rate.
 Downgrades: No charge or credit; effective next billing cycle.
 Annual to monthly switch: Allowed only at annual renewal date.
Section 5: Technical Troubleshooting
5.1 Login Issues
Issue: Cannot log in with correct credentials
Solution: Clear browser cookies and cache. Try an incognito/private window. Check if Caps Lock is on. If using
SSO, verify your organization's SSO configuration. Reset password if needed. If issue persists after reset,
contact support.
Issue: Account locked after failed attempts
Solution: Wait 30 minutes for automatic unlock. Alternatively, use 'Forgot Password' to reset credentials, which
also unlocks the account immediately.
Issue: 2FA code not working
Solution: Ensure device time is synchronized (2FA codes are time-based). If using SMS, check for signal.
Backup codes are available in Settings > Security. Contact support to disable 2FA if backup codes are lost.
Issue: SSO login failing
Solution: Verify the SSO domain in Settings > Security > SSO Configuration. Ensure the user's email domain
matches the configured SSO domain. Check IdP (Identity Provider) logs for errors. Common IdPs: Okta, Azure
AD, Google Workspace.
5.2 Performance Issues
Issue: App loading slowly
Solution: Check internet connection speed (minimum recommended: 5 Mbps). Disable browser extensions.
Clear cache. Try a different browser. Check status.novatech.io for ongoing incidents.
Issue: File uploads failing
Solution: Verify file size is within plan limits (Core: 25 MB/file, Pro: 100 MB/file, Business/Enterprise: 500
MB/file). Check storage quota in Settings > Storage. Supported formats: PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PNG, JPG, MP4,
ZIP. Corrupt files may fail silently.
Issue: Notifications not working
Solution: Check notification settings in Settings > Notifications. Ensure the NovaTech domain (novatech.io) is
not blocked by email/spam filters. Browser notifications require permission to be granted in browser settings.
Issue: Real-time sync not updating
Solution: Refresh the page. Check network stability. If using mobile, ensure background app refresh is
enabled. Real-time sync requires a stable WebSocket connection (port 443).
5.3 Integration Troubleshooting
Issue: Slack integration not posting
Solution: Re-authorize the Slack app in Settings > Integrations > Slack. Ensure the bot has permission to post
in the target channel. Check that the workspace admin has not restricted third-party app installs.
Issue: GitHub sync not working
Solution: Verify repository permissions. The GitHub token must have repo and webhook scopes. Check if the
organization has OAuth app restrictions enabled. Re-connect by revoking and re-authorizing under Settings >
Integrations > GitHub.
Issue: Zapier/API returning 401 errors
Solution: API key may be expired or revoked. Generate a new key in Settings > API > Manage Keys. Verify the
API base URL is https://api.novatech.io/v2. Rate limits: Core=0, Pro=1,000 req/hr, Business=10,000 req/hr,
Enterprise=custom.
5.4 Data Issues
 Deleted items are moved to Trash and recoverable within 30 days (Core: 7 days).
 Permanent deletion occurs after trash retention period; not recoverable.
 Data exports available in Settings > Data > Export (JSON or CSV format).
 If data appears missing after a plan downgrade, it is archived, not deleted. Upgrade to re-access.
 Bulk import via CSV supported for tasks and contacts. Template available at novatech.io/templates.
Section 6: Returns, Refunds & Cancellations
6.1 Refund Policy
NovaTech operates a 30-day money-back guarantee for first-time subscribers on Pro and Business plans. The
30-day period begins on the date of initial subscription. Refund requests must be submitted via
support@novatech.io or through the in-app chat. Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days to
the original payment method.
6.2 Non-Refundable Situations
 Renewals (monthly or annual) after the 30-day window has passed.
 Add-on purchases (AI Assistant, NovaAnalytics) after 7 days of purchase.
 Accounts suspended for Terms of Service violations.
 Partial months when cancelling mid-cycle (monthly plan).
 Promotionally priced or discounted subscriptions (unless stated otherwise).
6.3 Cancellation Process
To cancel a subscription, go to Settings > Billing > Cancel Plan. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the
current billing period. Data is retained for 30 days post-cancellation before permanent deletion. Users will
receive a confirmation email within 10 minutes of cancellation. Annual plan cancellations within 30 days receive
a prorated refund for unused months. After 30 days, annual plans are non-refundable but remain active until the
annual period ends.
6.4 Disputed Charges
If a customer does not recognize a charge, they should first check the invoice history in Settings > Billing. If the
charge is unclear, contact support with the invoice number, transaction date, and amount. NovaTech will
investigate within 2 business days. Opening a credit card chargeback without first contacting NovaTech support
may result in account suspension during investigation.
Section 7: Data Privacy & Security
7.1 Data Storage and Location
Customer data is stored on AWS infrastructure. Default region is US-East-1. Business and Enterprise
customers can select their preferred data region: US (us-east-1), EU (eu-west-1), APAC (ap-southeast-1). Data
residency selection is available during onboarding or via request to your account manager.
7.2 Compliance Certifications
 SOC 2 Type II: Audited annually. Certificate available on request.
 ISO 27001: Certified. Last audit: January 2025.
 GDPR: Fully compliant. DPA (Data Processing Agreement) available for Business and Enterprise.
 HIPAA: Business Associate Agreement (BAA) available for Enterprise customers.
 CCPA: Compliant. Privacy policy updated February 2025.
7.3 Data Encryption
All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher. Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256. Encryption
keys are managed via AWS KMS. Enterprise customers may request customer-managed encryption keys
(CMEK).
7.4 GDPR Data Subject Requests
 Right to Access: Submit via Settings > Privacy > Download My Data or email privacy@novatech.io.
 Right to Erasure: Email privacy@novatech.io with subject 'Erasure Request'. Response within 30 days.
 Right to Portability: Data exported in JSON or CSV format within 30 days.
 Right to Rectification: Update personal data in Settings > Profile or contact support.
 Data Retention: Personal data retained for duration of account plus 30 days post-deletion.
7.5 Security Incidents
In the event of a security breach affecting customer data, NovaTech will notify affected customers within 72
hours as required by GDPR. Notifications are sent to the account owner's email address. Security advisories
are published at security.novatech.io. To report a vulnerability, email security@novatech.io.

Section 9: Escalation & Human Handoff Protocol
9.1 When to Escalate to a Human Agent
The AI support assistant should escalate to a human agent in any of the following situations. Escalation
ensures customer trust, compliance, and satisfaction on complex or sensitive issues.
 Billing disputes involving charges over $200 or suspected fraudulent transactions.
 Account recovery requests where identity verification is required.
 Legal or compliance inquiries, including GDPR data deletion requests.
 Customers expressing frustration, anger, or explicitly requesting a human agent.
 Security incident reports or suspected data breaches.
 Enterprise contract negotiations, renewals, or custom pricing requests.
 Queries involving potential Terms of Service violations or account suspension appeals.
 Technical issues not resolved after two troubleshooting attempts.
 Any situation where the AI has low confidence in its response (below 0.65 threshold).
 Requests involving sensitive personal or health information.

Section 10: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
Q1. How do I upgrade my plan?
Go to Settings > Billing > Change Plan. Select your desired plan and confirm. Upgrades take effect immediately
with prorated billing. You can also contact support for assistance.
Q2. Can I use NovaTech offline?
NovaTech requires an internet connection for real-time features. The desktop app caches recent data for offline
viewing, but editing is disabled offline. Changes made offline sync automatically when reconnected.
Q3. How do I add team members?
Go to Settings > Team > Invite Members. Enter email addresses and assign roles. Invitees receive an email
with a join link valid for 7 days. On the Core plan, maximum team size is 5 members.
Q4. What happens to my data if I cancel?
Data is retained for 30 days after cancellation. You can export your data in Settings > Data > Export before the
retention period ends. After 30 days, all data is permanently deleted.
Q5. Can I have multiple workspaces?
Yes. Pro users can create up to 3 workspaces. Business users can create unlimited workspaces. Each
workspace is billed separately unless consolidated under an Enterprise account.
Q6. How do I export project data?
Go to Settings > Data > Export. Choose format (JSON or CSV) and select the projects to export. Export files
are generated within 10 minutes and emailed to the account owner.
Q7. Does NovaTech integrate with Google Workspace?
Yes. NovaTech integrates with Google Calendar, Google Drive, and Gmail. Set up under Settings >
Integrations > Google Workspace. Requires Google account authorization. Available for Pro and above.
Q8. What is the maximum file size I can upload?
Core: 25 MB per file. Pro: 100 MB per file. Business: 500 MB per file. Enterprise: Custom (up to 5 GB with
configuration). Total storage per plan: Core 5 GB, Pro 50 GB, Business 200 GB, Enterprise custom.
Q9. How does the AI Assistant work?
The AI Assistant uses your project data to suggest task priorities, detect blockers, summarize meeting notes,
and auto-schedule tasks. It does not share your data with third parties. AI features can be disabled in Settings >
AI > Disable AI Features.
Q10. How do I reset my 2FA?
If you have backup codes, use one at login and reconfigure 2FA in Settings > Security. If backup codes are
unavailable, contact support@novatech.io with account verification details. Identity verification will be required
before 2FA is reset.
Q11. Can I switch from monthly to annual billing?
Yes. Go to Settings > Billing > Switch to Annual. You will be charged the annual rate immediately, minus credit
for the unused portion of your current monthly cycle. The annual period begins immediately.
Q12. Is there a student or nonprofit discount?
Yes. Verified students and nonprofit organizations receive a 50% discount on Pro plans. Submit verification
documents to discounts@novatech.io. Approved discounts apply within 3 business days.
Q13. How do I contact support?
Core plan users: community.novatech.io. Pro users: support@novatech.io. Business users: Email + live chat at
app.novatech.io. Enterprise: Dedicated phone line provided at onboarding.
Q14. What browsers are supported?
Chrome (v90+), Firefox (v88+), Safari (v14+), Edge (v90+). Internet Explorer is not supported. For the best
experience, use the latest version of Chrome or Firefox.
Q15. How long does it take to process a refund?
Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days to the original payment method. For PayPal,
processing may take up to 5 additional days. A confirmation email is sent once the refund is initiated.
